# Revised Cinematic Portfolio Trailer Plan

## Production definition

The revised launch trailer is a **39-second, 9:16 vertical, 24 fps** Instagram Story/Reel for Jainam Jain. It is an original, physically believable technology-film teaser built around the verified Signal Edition portfolio, FocusPath project framing, and the live portfolio domain `jainamfolio-q6eaygh3.manus.space`. It uses no narration, no fabricated achievements, and no third-party intellectual property.

The visual language is **editorial science-fiction thriller**: deep black and charcoal, restrained white interface light, the existing ember orange brand accent, practical-looking black glass, real-screen illumination, floating dust, shallow focus, and controlled camera motion. The style intentionally avoids stock footage, rainbow gradients, aggressive neon, overbuilt CGI, generic corporate animation, or fictional product claims.

## Transition rules

Each generated clip keeps a physically present vertical black-glass display, atmospheric dust, and a restrained ember reflection within the frame. The montage can cut between display views; all other scene changes follow a practical screen-reflection, haze, or black-frame transition. The URL only appears in the final six seconds, beneath the invitation, as a small legible premium end-card line rather than as the core message.

## Separate BGM blueprint

The original soundtrack is an instrumental, 39-second, 76 BPM cinematic technology score in **D minor**. Core DNA: dark acoustic-electric hybrid score; distant analog drone, soft sub-bass, bowed-metal texture, low piano resonance, dry tonal tick, and one restrained orchestral-impact layer; high-resolution studio mix with vast dark-room reverb. No vocals, no lyrics, no EDM, no corporate music, and no imitation of existing film soundtracks.
